# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are prospective clients and employers evaluating Drew Sepeczi for product design and software engineering work.

They need to quickly understand what Drew has built, judge the quality and relevance of that work, and decide whether to contact or hire him.

## Product Purpose

Pontail is Drew Sepeczi's product portfolio. It demonstrates proven work across AI products, developer tools, full-stack web applications, and interface design studies.

Success means the portfolio gives prospective clients and employers enough credible evidence to continue to the showcased work, visit the broader portfolio, or make contact.

## Positioning

The portfolio is grounded in shipped work rather than speculative capability. Its central claim is that Drew designs and builds software people can actually use, carrying useful workflows from concept through production.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan a concise homepage, review selected product cards, open live product links, explore interface studies, and use the portfolio or contact links when they want deeper evidence or a conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing selected products, product descriptions, interface studies, destinations, and contact paths unless Drew explicitly updates them.
- Preserve the current claims that the selected products are shipped and active.
- Treat live products and existing interface studies as the portfolio's primary proof.
- Keep future work focused on a personal portfolio for prospective clients and employers; do not turn it into a generic agency or product marketplace.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Drew Sepeczi.
- Location: Chicago.
- Core statement: “I design and build software people can actually use.”
- Voice: concise, restrained, direct, and evidence-led.
- Preserve the incumbent identity and current content unless a future request explicitly authorizes a redesign or copy change.

## Evidence on Hand

- Selected product records and live destinations in `app/page.tsx`.
- Product artwork and supporting visual components in `app/product-card-visual.tsx` and `public/`.
- Interface studies implemented as routes under `app/`, with screenshots under `public/screenshots/`.
- Portfolio, GitHub, LinkedIn, and contact destinations in `app/page.tsx`.
- No testimonials, client logos, performance benchmarks, or other third-party proof have been confirmed; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with proven work.
2. Make quality and relevance understandable at a glance.
3. Keep the path from evidence to contact direct.
4. Describe capabilities concretely and without inflated claims.
5. Preserve human ownership of the work and its presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The portfolio should remain keyboard-accessible, responsive across desktop and mobile web, respectful of reduced-motion preferences, and understandable without relying on motion or color alone.
