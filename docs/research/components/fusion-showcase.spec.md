# FusionShowcase Specification

## Hero header

- Clone the responsive source hero instead of reducing it to text over a static screenshot.
- At roughly 1167px wide, the source hero is 1219px tall. Its 810px content column starts near x=178; the eyebrow begins at y=170, the 58/63.8px headline at y=212, the body at y=427, and CTAs at y=519.
- Use the source's looping abstract streak video as a decorative, muted, autoplaying background beneath dark readability gradients.
- Recreate the prompt composer with real HTML: GPT 5.5 selector, globe button, animated prompt, three mode chips, and a gradient Send button.
- Place the source dashboard artwork beneath the composer at its 2280:1098 aspect ratio. Do not substitute an unrelated sidebar mockup.
- Keep and responsively crop the layered visual on mobile rather than hiding it.
- Reduced motion pauses decorative motion and displays the complete prompt.
