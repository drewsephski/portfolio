# Fusion AI Behaviors

- Navigation is fixed at the top and overlays the page. Desktop exposes links and CTA; mobile reduces to brand plus menu control.
- Primary buttons use a layered-label hover animation; cards use subtle border/glow changes.
- Hero text and imagery reveal on load. Section content reveals as it enters the viewport.
- The product preview is time/click driven across Workflow, Analytics, and Integration imagery.
- Testimonial cards move horizontally in a looping rail.
- FAQ questions are click-driven accordions; the second item was expanded in the observed desktop state.
- Desktop bento and alternating layouts stack into a single column on mobile. Section padding changes from `100px 40px` to `50px 16px`.
- No page-level scroll snap was observed. Overflow is clipped selectively for glow, slider, and dashboard compositions.
